FAQ

Questions, answered

Do I need a credit card to start?

Nope. The Free plan is genuinely free for your first 3 clients — no card, no trial countdown, no surprises.

Can my clients pay through Settle?

Yes. Connect Stripe or PayPal and clients can pay straight from the invoice with one click. Funds land in your account, not ours.

What do the automatic reminders look like?

Friendly, on-brand emails sent on your schedule before and after the due date. You set the tone; Settle handles the timing.

Can I cancel anytime?

Absolutely. Cancel in two clicks from your dashboard — no calls, no retention maze. You keep access until the end of your billing period.
